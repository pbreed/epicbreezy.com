78 legs and roughly 6,700 nm flown so far, in about 116 hours airborne. The route has wandered from the original plan — Segment 2 turned east along the Gulf instead of returning to Oceanside, and Segment 3 ran up the Atlantic seaboard to Maine before heading west.

Segment 1 — Done

Five flying days, twelve legs, southbound border run from Oceanside, CA to Brownsville, TX:

  • Tue 4/14 — KOKB → KCXL → E63 → E95 (Oceanside to Benson, AZ via the border)
  • Wed 4/15 — E95 → KLRU → KVHN → E38 (Arizona desert to West Texas)
  • Thu 4/16 — E38 → T89 → KOZA (through the heart of Big Bend National Park)
  • Fri 4/17 — KOZA → KUVA → KCOT (overcast cruise on top of the layer)
  • Sat 4/18 — KCOT → KBKS → KBRO (SpaceX Starbase flyby into Brownsville)

Trip totals: ~22.5 hours airborne, three video cameras (left wing, right wing, forward "Eye of God"), live-streamed via Starlink to @PaulBreed on YouTube.

Download the actual GPS track: Segment1_Trip.kml — opens in Google Earth, shows the flown path of all 12 legs with altitude profile and airport markers. ~1.8 MB.

Segment 2 — Done

Brownsville east along the Gulf coast to Florida, then a loop down the peninsula and back. Seven flying days, nineteen legs:

  • Fri 6/5 — KBRO → KRAS … KTFP → KGLS → KORG (Texas coast to the Louisiana line)
  • Sat 6/6 — KORG → KIYA → KGAO (down into the Louisiana bayous)
  • Sun 6/7 — KGAO → 5R2 → KECP → X13 (Mississippi and the Florida panhandle)
  • Mon 6/8 — X13 → KCTY → KCGC (across the Big Bend to Crystal River)
  • Tue 6/9 — three short rides out of KCGC
  • Fri 6/12 — KCGC → KVNC → KMKY → X51 → KMTH (down the west coast to Marathon)
  • Sat 6/13 — KMTH → X51 → X26 → KCGC (back up the east coast)

~30 hours airborne. The KRAS → KTFP hop was flown but not filmed.

Departure had been held for Starship — the plan was to time the trip around the next Boca Chica launch window and overfly Starbase on the way out. In the event the outbound track passed about 6 nm north of the pad at 1,250 ft, 17 minutes after leaving Brownsville. The route as planned is still on SkyVector — compare it with the KML below to see where the trip actually went.

Download the actual GPS track: Segment2_Trip.kml — 19 legs coloured by day, with airport markers. ~2.9 MB.

Segment 3 — Done

The long one: Florida to Montana the scenic way, up the Atlantic seaboard to the Canadian border, west across the Great Lakes to Oshkosh, then out across the northern plains. Sixteen flying days, forty-seven legs, ~63 hours airborne.

  • Jul 2-5 — KCGC up through Georgia and the Carolinas to KBLM, New Jersey
  • Jul 8-9 — New England to KFVE, Frenchville, Maine, on the Canadian border
  • Jul 10-13 — west through Vermont, New York, Ontario's south shore and Michigan
  • Jul 15-18 — across Michigan and Lake Michigan to KOSH for AirVenture
  • Jul 22-24 — Wisconsin and Minnesota to North Dakota, then west to KGTF, Great Falls

Three flights on this segment were flown but not filmed — two overnight repositions, and the short hop from Walhalla to Cavalier, North Dakota after Walhalla turned out to have no fuel.

Download the actual GPS track: Segment3_Trip.kml — 47 legs coloured by day, with airport markers. ~6.1 MB.

Segment 2 and 3 tracks were reconstructed from the wing-camera GPS telemetry rather than a filed flight plan — every landing resolves to a real airport, and the timings agree with the ADS-B record to a minute or two.
